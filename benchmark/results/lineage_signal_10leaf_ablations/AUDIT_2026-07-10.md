## Code fixes and configuration corrections

- Updated the ablation rerun-completeness check so a run is considered complete only when every target has a diagnostic JSON and every diagnostic reports success.
- Corrected `benchmark/configs/lineage_signal_10leaf_10rep.json` so `wrong_fate_map_mode` matches the actually used stress-test condition: `cross_clade_short_branches`.
- Kept the wrong-fate-map condition explicitly as a strong negative control: cross-clade topology scrambling plus branch lengths compressed to `0.001`.
- Fixed NumPy compatibility in `regarch/gene_regulatory_network.py` by replacing unsupported `np.reshape(..., newshape=...)` calls with positional shape arguments.

## Metric audit

- `Precision at true K` uses `K = true_edge_count` for each evaluated node.
- For the current leaf-node evaluations, `K` ranges from 11 to 23, with mean 16.68.
- `Signed precision at true K` counts top-K predictions that are true edges and have the correct inferred sign, divided by K.
- Sparse method outputs are evaluated on the full non-self regulator-target universe; missing scores are filled with zero.
- Missing signs are treated as unavailable rather than as randomly guessed signs.

## Result audit

Recomputed `ablation_group_summary.tsv` from `ablation_metrics.tsv`.
Maximum absolute differences from the reported summary were floating-point roundoff only:

- AUPRC mean: `1.11e-16`
- AUROC mean: `3.33e-16`
- Precision at true K mean: `0`
- Signed precision at true K mean: `0`

Leaf-node means:

| condition | AUROC | AUPRC | Precision at true K | Signed precision |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| full | 0.938452 | 0.679058 | 0.600803 | 0.561916 |
| wrong_fate_map | 0.905268 | 0.636690 | 0.565726 | 0.530553 |
| no_fate_map | 0.894066 | 0.625902 | 0.583937 | 0.535376 |

Paired replicate-level one-sided Wilcoxon tests for leaf-node means:

| comparison | metric | mean full-minus-ablation | p-value |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| full vs no_fate_map | AUPRC | 0.053155 | 0.004883 |
| full vs no_fate_map | AUROC | 0.044386 | 0.000977 |
| full vs no_fate_map | Precision at true K | 0.016866 | 0.116211 |
| full vs no_fate_map | Signed precision | 0.026540 | 0.215820 |
| full vs wrong_fate_map | AUPRC | 0.042368 | 0.006836 |
| full vs wrong_fate_map | AUROC | 0.033184 | 0.000977 |
| full vs wrong_fate_map | Precision at true K | 0.035077 | 0.009766 |
| full vs wrong_fate_map | Signed precision | 0.031362 | 0.013672 |

Interpretation: full is consistently higher in the means. The wrong-fate-map stress test shows significant degradation across all four leaf metrics. The no-fate-map condition shows clear degradation for AUROC and AUPRC, while Precision at true K and signed precision degrade more modestly in this rerun.
